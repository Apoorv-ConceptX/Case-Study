01 · The challenge

Millions of poles.
Manual inspection can't keep up.

Our engineering partner on this programme delivers inspection services to major US utilities, including the distribution network covered by this case study. Traditional workflows — field crews, manual photo review, spreadsheet-based reporting — were slow, inconsistent, and expensive to scale across thousands of route miles.

Slow expert review cycles

Engineers manually screening tens of thousands of drone images per feeder, image by image.

Inconsistent defect grading

Severity judgements varying between reviewers, making programme-level risk ranking unreliable.

No living asset inventory

Pole-level component data trapped in PDFs and folders — impossible to audit against as-built records.

Multi-source data chaos

Optical, thermal, LiDAR and GPS streams from every flight — with no single platform to fuse them.
